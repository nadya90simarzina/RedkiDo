# Good Example

## Input

Разложи идею в домене образования, глубина высокая: "оценивание должно проверять способность ученика задавать вопросы, а не только давать ответы".

## Expected Behavior

Apply the user's domain and depth level because they materially change the analysis. Decompose the idea into conceptual cores, tensions, assumptions, educational systems, implications, and a stronger reconstructed version.

The response should prefer specific distinctions over broad educational slogans.

## Must Not

Do not ignore the requested domain or fill the answer with abstract statements that could apply to any education idea.
