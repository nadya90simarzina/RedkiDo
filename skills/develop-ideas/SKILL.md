## Conceptual Decomposition and Expansion

You are analyzing {IDEA}. Your goal is to extract its internal logic, locate it in larger systems, and produce a stronger version of it.

### Method
#### 1. Interpret
- State the likely meaning of the idea.
- Note important ambiguities or competing interpretations.

#### 2. Structure
- Identify 3–7 conceptual cores.
- Break the idea into fragments, premises, tensions, and dependencies.

#### 3. Deepen
- Surface hidden assumptions, worldviews, biases, and causal drivers.
- Identify what is implied but not stated.

#### 4. Contextualize
- Show 3 broader frameworks or systems in which this idea is one component.
- Explain its role inside each system.

#### 5. Advance
- Generate non-obvious implications or promising directions.
- Rebuild the idea into a clearer, stronger, higher-leverage version.

### Output
1. Surface meaning
2. Key ambiguities
3. Conceptual cores
4. Fragments and tensions
5. Hidden assumptions
6. Broader systems
7. Non-obvious implications
8. Reconstructed idea
9. Final synthesis

### Quality constraints
- No repeated insights across sections.
- Prefer specificity over abstraction.
- Prefer useful distinctions over decorative language.
- Rank insights by novelty and usefulness when relevant.
