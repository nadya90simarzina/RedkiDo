# Good Example: Marketplace Bedspread Try-On

## Input

Первое фото - моя спальня. Второе фото - покрывало с маркетплейса. Положи это покрывало на кровать на первом фото.

## Expected Behavior

Use the first image as the room target and the second image as the product reference. Ignore the product photo background. Preserve the bed position, room geometry, lighting, and camera angle. Place the bedspread naturally on the existing bed with believable drape, folds, thickness, occlusion, and shadows while preserving the product's color, pattern, material, and recognizable design.

## Must Not

Do not paste the product image as a flat rectangle, do not keep the marketplace background, do not change the bed frame or room, and do not invent a different pattern or color.
