# Anti-Example: No Rectangular Product Paste

## Input

Вот фото комнаты и скрин товара с маркетплейса. Поставь этот коврик на пол перед кроватью.

## Expected Behavior

Use the product as an object reference only. Remove or ignore the marketplace background mentally, align the rug to the floor perspective, scale it naturally, tuck it under nearby furniture if appropriate, and add realistic shadows and texture.

## Must Not

Do not paste the screenshot rectangle into the room, do not include marketplace text, buttons, prices, watermarks, or white background, and do not distort the room perspective.
