# Good Example: Add Nightstands

## Input

Вот фото моей спальни. Добавь прикроватные тумбочки по обе стороны кровати, в спокойном современном стиле.

## Expected Behavior

Treat this as insert-only. Add two plausible nightstands scaled to the bed height and room perspective, with realistic contact shadows and matching light direction. Preserve the bed, bedding, walls, floor, windows, existing objects, camera angle, and crop.

## Must Not

Do not redesign the whole room, replace the bed, change the bedding, alter the walls/floor/windows, or add unrelated furniture.
