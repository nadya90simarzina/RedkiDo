# Good Example: Home Staging For Rent

## Input

Прикрепляю фото спальни. Сделай хоумстейджинг для аренды, уютно и современно, но окна, стены и пол не менять.

## Expected Behavior

Use the room photo as the edit target. Preserve geometry, camera angle, crop, walls, floor, windows, doors, fixed furniture, lighting, and shadows. Add realistic sale-friendly bedding, small decor, a rug, lamps, plants, or wall art only where plausible. Keep the result photorealistic and broad-appeal, not a fantasy showroom.

## Must Not

Do not move windows, change flooring, repaint walls, invent new doors, widen the room, remove personal items unless explicitly requested, or alter people/pets if present.
